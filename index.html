Meet the Developer

Varun K.

Software Developer Android Craftsman India 🇮🇳

Hi, I'm Varun K — a software developer from India who loves building fast, beautiful apps that respect the user's time. I focus on clean architecture, thoughtful motion design, and interfaces that feel alive but not noisy. Notflix (rebranded here as VelvetVault) is my take on a premium streaming shell: lightweight, offline-capable, and carefully tuned.

When I'm not coding, I'm probably tweaking gradients, reading about ExoPlayer internals, or shipping small experiments. Follow my journey and connect with me on Instagram — I share builds, behind-the-scenes, and dev logs there.

The Mission

Build streaming tools that feel premium and run lean — no bloat, no noise, just playback.

The Stack

Kotlin · Jetpack Compose · Media3 · Room · Hilt · custom M3U parsing · clean MVVM.

The Craft

Espresso-and-amber dark theme, micro-interactions, and a focus on real-device performance.
